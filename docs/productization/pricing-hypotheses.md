# Pricing hypotheses

These proposals are not validated willingness to pay or active subscriptions.

| Plan | Proposed price/month | Conservative future monthly entitlements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $299 | 1 project, 100 imports, 50 approved cases, 500 evaluations, 1 GB |
| Team | $799 | 3 projects, 500 imports, 250 approved cases, 3,000 evaluations, 5 GB |
| Business | $1,999 | 10 projects, 2,000 imports, 1,000 approved cases, 10,000 evaluations, 20 GB |

Do not sell unlimited model use or unlimited storage. Customer CI compute and model
API charges are excluded until measured and explicitly packaged. Adjust entitlement
configuration centrally, not throughout business logic. The first offline slice
uses operator caps unrelated to paid tiers and cannot assert a subscription state.

Validate $799 by paid pilot/continuation, measurable time saved and repeat usage.
Record objections about privacy, verifier accuracy, integration work, price and
budget owner. Discount only with an explicit end date; do not count a free pilot
as ARR. Stripe test-mode integration comes later, and live transactions require
operator approval. Never handle card data in this application.
